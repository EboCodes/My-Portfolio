About me

Craft over convenience.

I write code the way a craftsman builds furniture — with intention, patience, and attention to what will last.

I'm a full-stack web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria with 3+ years of experience building websites and applications for businesses ready to grow online.

My work spans the full stack — from pixel-perfect front-end interfaces to solid back-end systems. I care about performance, accessibility, and the kind of detail that separates a good website from a great one.

I've worked across industries: real estate, legal services, e-commerce, fintech, wellness, photography and more. Each project taught me something. That experience is what I bring to yours.
